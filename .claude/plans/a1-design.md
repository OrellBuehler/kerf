# A1 design: FrameSource and a complete RenderPlan

Phase 1 of A1 (`feat/gpu-a1`, off `f14e06d`), revised after critique. Design only. Names are in `crates/kerf-core/src/engine/cli.rs` unless stated.

**Measured** (FFmpeg 6.1.1 and pinned 9.0.2 unless noted; lossless 10 fps frame-number clips, luma = 16 + 4n):
1. A y4m pipe **without `-fps_mode passthrough` duplicates VFR frames to constant rate** (120 packets became 179).
2. `-vf showinfo` before `scale` prints one `n: pts:` line per frame, 1:1 with stdout, plus `config in time_base: a/b`. **Plain `showinfo` checksums every frame: +65% decode time on 6.1, +40% on 9.0.2; `showinfo=checksum=0` is free.** `pts_time` keeps 6 digits, so identity is the **integer pts in ticks**. `-copyts -start_at_zero -ss T` gives absolute pts, relative to the file's `start_time` (as `-ss` is). **`-ss` rounds to the nearest tick** (1/24: `-ss 1.02` returns the 1.000 s frame).
3. **`-ss {:.3}` skips a frame**: with a frame at 1.0006, `-ss 1.0006` returns it, the spelling `1.001` returns the next.
4. The frame the export shows is **not** "first frame >= T". Source frames 2..7: **reverse `[7,6,5,4,3,2]`** (the still's `-ss 0.8` picks 8); speed 0.5 `[2,2,3,3,4,4,5,5]`; speed 2 `[2,4,6,8]`; speed 1.5 `[2,4,5,7,8,10]`; a clip placed 0.7 of a frame late `[2,2,3,4,5]`, 0.3 late `[2,3,4,5]`; reverse at speed 2 `[9,7,5,3]`. All equal **the last frame with `pts < ws + s*((k+1/2)/fps - start)`** (`ws` window start, `s` speed, `start` timeline start, `k` output frame; the first frame if none), reverse being that rule over the window, then mirrored (`P0 + Plast - pts`).
5. mpegts seeks sloppily (`-ss 1.0` delivered the 2.002 s keyframe first). **Both builds, not 6.1 only** (re-measured on 6.1.1 and 9.0.2 with a GOP of 15: `-ss 1.0` gives frame 31 of a 30 fps numbered clip, `-ss 1.2` frame 43 for 36, `-ss 2.7` frame 91 for 81, identically on both). An all-intra transport stream is exact, which is why `picked.rs` writes `.ts` with `-g 1`; a long-GOP one may not deliver the frame the pick names, whatever the build.
6. Proxies are build-dependent. VFR: 6.1 holds frames to constant rate, 9.0.2 keeps them. **Video starting 5 s after the container: 9.0.2's proxy keeps that start, so `-ss T` on the proxy is `-ss T+5` on the original** (6.1's agreed). Pre-existing in FFmpeg's own preview.
7. A proxy is a smaller picture than the probed stream: `Project::preview_assets` swaps `path` but keeps the original's `StreamInfo` (right for the graph; wrong for A0's y4m size check and `LayerGeometry`).
8. `fps=29.97` is the grid 2997/100, `fps=29.97002997002997` is 30000/1001; `nominal_fps` snaps jittery VFR to the former.
9. Costs (6.1): one-shot 90 ms (720p all-intra proxy) / 260 ms (1080p GOP 120); streaming 3.6 / 2.5 ms a frame.
10. **FFmpeg evaluates graph time at `t = k * (den / num)`** (`pts * av_q2d(time_base)`, doubles), which is not `k / fps` and not `k * den / num`: one ulp *below* the exact time for 33 % of frames at 24 fps, 50 % at 29.97, 8.6 % at 30 and 60, none at 25 (13 % above). A clip that starts exactly on such a frame has `enable='between(t,s,e)'` false there and **loses its first frame**: 12 of 36 frame-aligned clips at 24 fps, 28 at 29.97, 4 at 30 and 60, 0 at 25 (6.1.1 and 9.0.2). Exact rationals are for the frame **pick** only (slot boundaries); every expression the graph evaluates uses `ffmpeg_frame_time`.
11. **What a clip is drawn on** (equal-rate, frame-aligned, rendered): red exactly when `start <= ffmpeg_frame_time(k) < end` — closed at the start, **open at the end** (the source has no frame at `end`), so `enabled(t)`, which includes `end` as `between` does, is necessary and not sufficient. A source *slower* than the export is held by the `fps` filter and **is** drawn on the frame at `end` for some clips (a 24 fps source in a 30 fps export, for one), and off-grid starts, speed != 1 and reverse differ again: that is `fps_pick`'s to reproduce, from `rendered.rs`'s pinned boundary cases (A1a-3).
12. **`fade` counts frames, it does not interpolate time.** It converts `st` and `d` (as `av_parse_time` reads the printed decimal: whole microseconds, truncated) to frames of its input time base, `S = round(st * fps)` and `N = max(1, round(d * fps))`, halves up, and frame `S + i` carries `i / N` (held outside): measured on both builds, off the grid by up to a frame, so `(t - st) / d` is wrong by up to `1 / N` and by a half-frame at the ends. Black and white dips and a dissolve's alpha ramp count alike. `FadeStep::progress_at_frame` is that model, within 3/255 of the rendered alpha.
13. The overlay places a slide/push at `(int)d & ~1` per axis (`d` the exact frame fraction times the frame size): truncated toward zero, then to the 4:2:0 chroma grid.
14. **A keyframed zoom is not what the graph's text says.** `scale eval=frame` sits *before* the chain's `fps`, so it is evaluated at the **source** frame's time: a 10 fps clip in a 30 fps export zooms in steps three output frames long (frame sizes after `fps`: `320x180` x3, `307x172` x3, ...). Position (`overlay` x/y), rotation and the `geq` opacity are after `fps` and read the output frame's time. And every filter after the zoom has to take a frame whose size changed mid-stream: the converter `overlay` gets in front of a `yuv420p` picture (scale-only keys: the chain ends `format=yuv420p`, overlay wants `yuva420p`) and `geq` (keyed opacity) hold the **first** frame's size for the whole clip, so the zoom never shows (red picture over green stays 320 wide at every frame); `format=yuva420p` alone lets it through; `rotate` after it jumps back to the full size and then to garbage, differently on 6.1 and 9.0. All on both builds (`a_keyframed_zoom_is_read_at_the_source_frame_and_filters_after_it_may_hold_it_still`). So the export and the preview stream, which shares the graph, do not animate a scale-only keyframed zoom today. Not fixed here (it would change argv and re-bless the oracle): the plan flags it (`Animated::zooms`, refused in Motion as `Unsupported::KeyedZoom` until `caps.keyed_zoom`), and which of "fix the graph" or "reproduce it" is the lead's call before A5.1.
15. The delivery rate is a rational the **spelling** picks: the graph prints `{}` of the `f64` and FFmpeg's `av_parse_video_rate` is `av_d2q(x, 1001000)`, so `29.97` runs on `2997/100` and `29.97002997002997` on `30000/1001`, `23.976` on `2997/125`, `0.5` on `1/2`. It is **two parses with different limits**: a `color=r=` source (the canvas, the overlay's clock) is `av_d2q(x, 1001000)` (`Rational::from_fps`), the `fps=` filter of each clip `av_d2q(x, INT_MAX)` (`Rational::from_fps_filter`; `PlanCanvas.fps` and `.pick_fps`) — `29.970029` is `92997/3103` on the canvas and `29970029/1000000` on a clip, and every small-ratio rate is the same on both. Both are ported and checked against `showinfo`'s `frame_rate:` for nineteen rates on both builds.
16. **Finding 4's rule is right to first order and wrong at the margins** (rendered, both builds, ~13,000 compared frames in `picked.rs`). The pick is integer/double arithmetic over the stream's own **time base**: `setpts` evaluates `(PTS-STARTPTS)/s + start/TB` in doubles and **truncates** to a tick (`D2TS` is an `(int64_t)` cast; rounding put a frame a slot *late* wherever its time lands a fraction of a tick under a half-slot boundary, e.g. frame 1 of 10 fps footage at speed 1.5 into 24 fps at phase 0.9); `fps` rescales to `1/fps` rounding half away from zero; `-ss` shifts by `rescale_q(seek_us + container_start_us)` rounded to a tick; `trim` keeps `[lo, hi)` with both ends truncated to whole microseconds (the export prints `{}`, the still `{:.6}`). A frame-aligned clip at speed 2 is *exactly* a tie, which `start/TB` truncation then breaks low — so the time base is an input, not a detail.
17. **The stream ends where the frame `trim` dropped would have landed** (the first frame at or past the window, retimed and rounded as any other; for a window to the end of the file, one frame interval — the file's last — past the last frame). From that slot on `fps` emits nothing and `overlay=eof_action=pass` draws nothing; before it, the last kept frame is held. That one rule is every closing-edge case: an equal-rate source is not drawn at `end`, a slower one is held for its whole share and *can* be drawn at `end`, a sped-up or reversed clip's last frame is dropped when its slot is at or past the end, and a one-frame window is one slot. It is the same through `reverse` (which forwards the EOF it is given) and for tails.
18. **`Timeline::slice` is not transparent at a transition from either side.** Besides zeroing the front-cut clip's `fade_in` and dropping its `transition_in`, it shortens the clip *before* the transition, and `transition_fx` clamps the transition to what is left of it: a dissolve shortens, a dip's fade-out restarts at the cut, and with the outgoing clip gone (`ce <= start`) the incoming one fades up from black instead of crossing it. So the windows a stream must not start in run from `lead` before the cut to the end of the fade-in, closed at the cut.
19. **`fade` on a layer with an alpha plane goes to luma 0, not 16** (6.1.1 and 9.0.2): `fade=t=out` on `yuv420p` ends at Y 16 (limited-range black), on `yuva420p` at Y 0 with the alpha untouched; a fade through white ends at 235 on both. A layer's chain carries an alpha plane whenever it has a chroma key, a mask, an opacity below 1, a rotation or a dissolve (`needs_alpha`), so **the black end of a `FadeStep` depends on the layer**: `FadeStep` / `LayerFx::strength` count frames correctly but describe a scalar towards "black", and must learn which black before A5.1 draws a fade (the plan's `PlanLayer` knows `needs_alpha`'s inputs; `strength` does not take them). Not modelled in A1a-3.
20. **A still image is a stream like any other** (rendered, both builds, 192 clips in `picked.rs`): the export reads it as `-loop 1 -framerate <fps> -t <end>`, a run of frames `0, 1, ...` on a `1/<fps>` time base cut off by the automatic `trim` of `-t`, no seek, the chain's own `trim` absolute (so `source_in` 0.25 starts the clip on the first frame at or after a quarter second). The frame that `-t` cuts at ends the stream exactly as in finding 17, so a still is **not** drawn on the frame its window closes on: the A1a-3 first cut, `AtOrAfter(0.0)` for a still image in a Motion plan, drew one extra frame at the closing edge of every still. The plan now gives a still `Pick::Fps` with `image: Some(<fps>)`, which makes the run up (it needs no frames from a `FrameSource`).
21. **The end of a window that runs to the end of the file is the last frame's own `duration`, not the last gap** (matroska: a 30 fps clip's gaps alternate 33 and 34 ticks and its last frame lasts 33; 26 of 300 clips were off with the gap). `SourceFrames.last_duration` carries it (ffprobe's frame `duration`, `showinfo`'s `duration:`); without it the last gap stands in.

## 1. FrameSource (kerf-gpu)

- **Run = one ffmpeg process decoding forward from a start point**, the unit of everything below: `-hide_banner -nostats -nostdin -loglevel info [-hwaccel h] -copyts -start_at_zero -ss <T:.6> -i <path> -an -sn -dn -map 0:v:0 -vf showinfo=checksum=0,scale=out_range=tv -fps_mode passthrough -f yuv4mpegpipe -pix_fmt yuv420p pipe:1`. A0's `decode_args` stays as reference and fallback; it and the still move to `{:.6}` together. The reader keeps **A0's size check** (mismatch: source `Unsupported` for 60 s) and reads straight into plane `Vec`s. A stderr thread parses only `] n:\s*(\d+) pts:\s*(-?\d+|NOPTS)`, pairs frame to line **by `n`** and re-reads every `config in time_base`; `NOPTS`, a changed time base or a count mismatch is fatal (kill, FFmpeg fallback). A **self-test on a real fine-time-base file** (production flags, exact pts expected) and the first spawn dying on an unknown flag (`-fps_mode`, `showinfo=checksum`: ffmpeg < 5.1) set a **process-wide "path disabled" flag**, in both roles.
- **Runs, not decoders.** Per file up to 3 runs, globally <= 6. A request goes to a run **behind it within `skip` frames** (reads forward, every frame cached); else it restarts the file's LRU run (spawning one under the caps, evicting the global LRU idle run). `skip` is **24 on a proxy, 96 on an original** (restart / frame cost, finding 9). Interactive backward restarts seek 15 frames early. **Prefetch** (`Forward`) starts a spare run at the next clip's first frame. **`Exact` (agent frames) never evicts a run**: one-shot or temporary run. **Thrash guard**: > 4 restarts/s on `Forward` is `Err(Busy)`, the caller falls back to `stream_preview`. `FrameCursor` (A7) is an exclusive run handle. Read-ahead is bounded **in bytes** (48 MiB a run): the reader blocks, the pipe fills, ffmpeg idles.
- **Identity and pick.** `FrameKey { source, pts }`: `source` = `fnv1a(source_key(path))` (path, size, mtime: a replaced file or new proxy never serves stale frames; proxy and original distinct) + `DecodeFormat` (`Yuv420p8`); `pts` in ticks. Two queries: `AtOrAfter(T)` = first frame with `pts >= round(T x tb)` (the still, `-ss`) and `Before(T)` = the frame preceding it (its `prev_pts`). Each cached frame stores `prev_pts` (a run's first: its rounded seek tick, clamped to one frame interval per finding 5), so both are one `BTreeMap` range query, exact on VFR. Motion's pick is finding 4's `fps` rule, **not** "the frame containing T" (wrong for reverse, speed != 1, phase >= 1/2). Past the last frame: `eof_after`, `Ok(None)` (draw nothing, as FFmpeg). Stills: one `-i` decode without `-ss`, cached.
- **Cache.** `BTreeMap<(source, pts), { Arc<YuvFrame>, prev_pts, tick }>`, byte-capped (256 MiB, `KERF_FRAME_CACHE_MB`), LRU by O(n) scan like `cli.rs`'s `FrameCache`; `Arc` frames outlive eviction while composited.
- **Proxy, original, 10-bit.** `FrameSource` decodes the `SourceMedia` it is handed (§2). **Stay on 8-bit y4m**: a 10-bit source is reduced where the graph reduces it (A0 `source/10-bit` 48.9 dB); HDR stays refused (A5.6); P010 only for A5.6 and A7 *10-bit delivery* (refused until then). No pre-scaling in ffmpeg (double scaling against the swscale port). **Proxy timing is bug-compatible**: the same `-ss` on the same proxy as FFmpeg's preview, so parity holds; fixture `proxy/late-video-start` pins finding 6 on both builds. The fix (offset by original minus proxy `format.start_time`) must also reach FFmpeg's preview: a separate WP, flagged.
- **hwaccel, CPU.** `decode_hwaccel()` at spawn; a run dying before its first frame is retried once in software, success calls a new `kerf_core::disable_decode_hwaccel()` (`HWACCEL_OK` is private today). Interactive role: **no `cpu::lease`**, normal priority, `limit_ffmpeg_args(args, live_runs + 1)` computed **at spawn**. Export role (A7): niced under the driver's reentrant lease.
- **Threads, lifetime, failure.** `Arc<FrameSource>` (Send + Sync), one per process, owned by the render service beside the project, never behind its lock; blocking calls (blocking-pool threads only) on a per-file mutex + condvar; `frames()` fans layers out with `thread::scope`; the same frame twice is one decode. The reaper holds a `Weak` and kills runs idle > 20 s; `release(source)`, `release_all()` (app exit) and `Drop` kill and wait every child. Deadlines: first frame 30 s, between frames 15 s (`PREVIEW_TIMEOUTS`), interactive request 5 s. `Decode` / `Unsupported` / timeout / `Busy`: the caller renders **that frame** (or span) through FFmpeg; `Ok(None)` is "no frame".
- **Traps a `FrameSource` must not step in** (found reviewing A1a-3; each is a way the pure pick and the pixels part):
  - `showinfo` pts under `-copyts -start_at_zero -ss` are **already relative to the container's start**: hand `SourceFrames` `start_us = 0` (a start that is not on a tick boundary can then differ from the export by a tick; that is the one place the pure reference is not exact).
  - Use the decoder's **best-effort timestamps** (what the filter graph sees, and what `showinfo` prints): some containers have no frame pts (AVI; ffprobe's `pts` is `N/A`).
  - A **streaming cursor** over a window needs `STARTPTS` (the first kept frame), one frame of lookahead, and the first frame past the window's end — or, when the file ends first, the last frame's `duration` (`SourceFrames.last_duration`): those fix the stream's end (finding 17).
  - **Reverse** needs *every* pts of the window (the forward timestamps are re-stamped onto the reversed frames, and `STARTPTS` is the first of them).
  - A **non-all-intra transport stream** may not deliver the frame the pick names after an `-ss` (finding 5, both builds): the pure pick says which frame the *filter chain* would choose among the frames it is given.
  - A still image needs no decode run at all beyond its one frame: the pick makes its frames up (`FpsPick::image`).
  - `Handover::frame` (below) must be passed on as the stream's `ExportOptions::resolution`.
- **Deferred to A2/A3**: pooled GPU textures, readback buffers, an upload cache.
- **As landed in A1b-1** (`frame_cache.rs`, `y4m.rs`, `showinfo.rs`, `router.rs` in kerf-gpu; `frame_pick.rs`, `engine/cli.rs` in kerf-core; all pure, nothing spawns). Differences from the text above:
  - **Cache coverage is `covers_from`, not `prev_pts`**: a frame answers `AtOrAfter(t)` for every tick in `covers_from..=pts` (a frame after another covers from the tick after it; a run's first from its seek tick, which the caller clamps to one frame interval — finding 5), `before(t)` is the entry at `covers_from - 1`, and `mark_end` makes later times `Lookup::PastEnd` (the design's `eof_after`). The cache is `BTreeMap<FrameKey, Entry>` with counted **pins** (a pinned frame is never evicted; the cap is soft for pins, `CacheStats` reports it), the frame just inserted is never its own victim, and a repeated insert keeps the first `Arc` and widens the coverage. `SourceId { file, format }` is `kerf_core::source_identity(path)` (a `u64`: `fnv1a(source_key)`) plus `DecodeFormat::Yuv420p8`; **`SourceMedia` / `PlanSource` still carry no identity** — `FrameSource` takes it from the decoded path when a frame is asked for (one `stat`; a replaced file is a new identity at the moment it matters).
  - **Y4m reader**: no `Option` size — `Y4mReader::new(reader, probed_size)` reads the header lazily with the first frame and compares **before allocating a plane**; layouts are the exact `C420` list (A0's `parse_y4m` accepts any `420*`, so `420p10` too — harmless there since `-pix_fmt yuv420p` is explicit, but not here).
  - **Showinfo**: the parser enforces `n` = 0, 1, 2, ... itself (so "pairing by `n`" is a queue index and a rebuilt graph, which renumbers, is an error) and carries the time base in force on each frame. A live cross-check of the production flag set against the binary is A1b-2's self-test.
  - **Router**: `Route::{Reuse, Start{evict, lead}, Restart{run, lead}, OneShot, Skip, Busy}`; **`Exact` is reuse-or-`OneShot`, it never starts a registered run**; `Prefetch` is a spare slot or `Skip`; the lead is `BACKWARD_LEAD` only when the target is behind *every* run of its file (a first request has no run to be behind). **The thrash guard counts run *replacements* (`Route::replaces`: `Restart`, or `Start` with an eviction), not starts into free slots**, or the first frame of a six-layer cut (six starts) would trip it; it is `ThrashGuard::restart(now)` with the clock passed in, `check(intent, route, now)` applies the `Forward`-only rule, and `Busy` converts to the new `GpuError::Busy`. The reuse window is two named constants, `REUSE_WINDOW_PROXY` (24) and `REUSE_WINDOW_ORIGINAL` (96).
  - **Streaming cursor rule**: `Pick::progress(&SourceFrames /*read so far*/, eof) -> PickProgress { NeedMore, NeedEarlier, Ready { shown, keep_from } }` in kerf-core (it needs `fps_pick`'s private run arithmetic: `fps_pick` was split into `window` / `run` / `place` with the same arithmetic, behaviour unchanged). Property-tested against `fps_pick` over the whole file for 4000 random pick/frame sets (never a different answer, answered by the end, and a forward pick no later than one frame past the shown one); mutation-checked (no lookahead, window complete without a frame past it or EOF, reverse not waiting). `FpsPick::seek()` is the run's `-ss`. `last_duration` is ignored until `eof`.
  - `disable_decode_hwaccel()` logs one `warn` the first time it flips the flag.
  - **After the A1b-1 review**:
    - The showinfo parser takes only lines with the `[Parsed_showinfo_N @ 0x…] ` prefix, including `config in time_base`, and strips ANSI colour first. A path or container title containing `] n:1 pts:0` no longer poisons it. `plain_log_env` is the spawn's half: it removes `AV_LOG_FORCE_COLOR` and sets `AV_LOG_FORCE_NOCOLOR=1`.
    - The reuse window is `effective_window`: `min(constant, cache_cap / frame_bytes / 2)`. At 1080p, a 96-frame read-forward no longer flushes the other layers. Frames a run only passes go in with `insert_cold`.
    - `ThrashGuard` is time-weighted. It refuses once restarts cost ≥ `THRASH_BUSY_SECS` (0.75 s) of the last second. Failed or empty runs count. A stale idle run on another file is not thrash.
    - Pins are `PinGuard`s, and a purge detaches them. `Before` at a run's first frame is `NeedEarlier`: only the cursor knows whether the run began at the file's start.
  - **Carried into A1b-2** (from the same review):
    - Capture the source identity at spawn and carry it in the run, not at lookup.
    - Empty stdout with a non-zero exit is a **failed start**, not EOF. Exit 0 with a header-only y4m is a seek past the end: `mark_end(source, seek_tick - 1)`.
    - Cap concurrent `Exact` one-shots with a semaphore.

## 2. RenderPlan, complete (kerf-core)

**Two modes.** `PlanMode::Still` is A0's contract (`build_still_args`); every A0 call and test unchanged. `PlanMode::Motion` is "the export graph at output frame `k`": `Planner::at_frame(k)`, with `PlanCanvas.fps` a **rational** parsed the way ffmpeg parses the `fps=` string the graph carries (finding 8) and `k*den/num` computed exactly **for the frame pick only** (NTSC slot-boundary tests): everything the graph evaluates at a frame — `enable`, a fade's start — uses FFmpeg's own `k*(den/num)` and frame counting (findings 10 to 12). Its deltas over the still, all in the code today: (1) outgoing clips play on `fx.tail`; (2) fades, dips, dissolve are timed from `timeline_start` with `dur` including the tail; (3) slide/push offsets join the position (`motion_expr`); (4) a keyframed clip never pads, always runs `scale eval=frame` (moving `second_scale`, hence `LayerGeometry`'s 4:2:0 point) and turns in a `hypot(iw,ih)` box when its keys rotate: `PlanLayer.animated`, and `LayerGeometry::resolve` gains `identity` and `motion` arguments whose defaults reproduce A0; (5) **keyframed opacity is a `geq` alpha, not the RGB round trip** (other arithmetic: refused until measured); (6) overlays are `between(t,start,end)`, end inclusive; (7) source frames follow the `fps` rule (finding 4), not `-ss`; (8) reframe is a `sendcmd` schedule (0.05 degree gate, held values, `cubic`; the still samples `line`), computed **once per clip in `Planner::new`**; (9) tone mapping sits after the fit scale, the still prefixes it; (10) `PlanCanvas` carries the output `pix_fmt` and gif: the GPU draws 8-bit 4:2:0 delivery only. The preview stream's `bilinear`/`yuvj420p` is an MJPEG-pipe artefact, **not** reproduced. Whether scrub adopts Motion is A3/A5.1's call (an unsupported frame falls back to a still that draws no fade).

**No transition node; transitions are per-layer, as in the graph.** `transition_fx` only decorates clips, so each side's layer carries its half: a dissolve is two ordinary layers (outgoing on its tail at alpha 1, incoming on an alpha ramp, or from black across a gap), a dip two fades (`d/2` a side), slide/push two offsets, no source handle a hard cut. `ClipFx` / `transition_fx` / `clip_source_window` / `clip_seek` move to a pure `clip_timing.rs` with `ClipTiming::{enabled, fades, motion_at}` (shipped in A1a-1; `pub(crate)`): the builders *format* from it, the plan *evaluates* it. `enabled` is the overlay's `enable` predicate, **necessary and not sufficient** — it is not "visible at `t`" (finding 11). `transition_fx`'s table is indexed by flat clip *storage* index over the `for_render()`ed timeline and the graph's own asset list, so the Planner reads it through `clips_with_fx` (`(track, index, clip, fx)`), never by a count of its own. `fades` is **video-only**: `audio_clip_chain` composes differently (`fi` adds `afade_in`, `fo` adds the tail) and stays put.

```rust
RenderPlan { time, mode, canvas: PlanCanvas /*+ fps: Rational, pix_fmt, policy*/, layers: Vec<PlanLayer>, overlays: Vec<PlanText> }
PlanLayer  { ..A0.., source: PlanSource { identity, proxy }, /* `stream` describes the DECODED file */ animated: bool,
             pick: Pick, fx: LayerFx, mask: Option<Mask> /*normalized*/, effects: Vec<VideoEffect> /*safe_color*/,
             reframe: Option<PlanReframe { pose, interp }>, hdr: Option<Hdr> }
Pick       { AtOrAfter(T) /*Still*/, Before(T), Fps { speed, reverse, window: (f64, f64), start, k } /*Motion*/ }
LayerFx    { fades: Vec<FadeStep { colour, st, d, progress }>, dissolve: Option<f32>, motion: (f64, f64), tail: bool }
PlanText   { id, text, size /*frame fraction*/, pos, alpha /*TextOverlay::sample*/, color, bg /*valid_color*/,
             font_file: Option<PathBuf> /*resolve_font_file; None is never drawn*/, synthetic_bold }
```
**As landed in A1a-2** (`planner.rs`, `plan_caps.rs`; differences from the sketch above): `PlanRequest { mode, color }` has no `media` yet (A1a-3 adds it with `SourceMedia`); `Rational` lives in `clip_timing.rs`; `RenderPlan` is `{ time, mode, frame, canvas, layers, overlays: Vec<PlanText>, pictureless }` and `PlanCanvas` gains `fps`, `pix_fmt`, `gif` and `policy` (so `reasons` is pure); `PlanLayer` gains `name`, `projection`, `hdr`, `effects`, `mask`, `reframe: Option<PlanReframe { pose, interp }>`, `fx`, `animated: Option<Animated { rotates, opacity, zooms }>` (the sketch's bool) and `timing: PlanTiming { window, source_window, speed, reversed }` — what `fps_pick` needs of a layer beyond the plan's `frame` and `canvas.fps`; `LayerFx` is `{ fades: Vec<FadeStep>, motion, tail }` (steps, per the last line of that paragraph; `strength(tint, frame, fps)` evaluates them) with no `dissolve` field (it is the `Alpha` tint's strength); `GpuCaps` has `motion` (Motion plans at all), `fades`, `transitions` (travel and tails), `keyed_opacity`, `keyed_zoom`, `mask`, `text`, `reframe`, `hdr` and an `EffectKinds` bitset, and a Motion plan is refused as a whole until `caps.motion`; `unsupported_reasons()` returns an owned `Vec<String>`. **`LayerGeometry` takes a `Placement { keyframed: Option<rotates>, offset }`** (`resolve_with`; `resolve` is `Placement::STILL`) instead of the sketch's `identity` / `motion` arguments: a keyframed clip is never identity, always has the second stage, boxes its rotation in `hypot(iw,ih)`, and the travel offset joins the position in the identity branch too. **Still refusals are exact now, and complete**: A0 refused any frame in `[start - d/2, start + d)` of a transition and missed the tail after the ramp. A still frame is refused while a fade step is live (`value < 1` at the frame on screen), a layer travels, or **a tail window is open** (`RenderPlan.tails`, `Unsupported::StillTail`: the export keeps drawing the outgoing clip until its window closes, after the ramp, around an incoming clip that does not cover it) — **whatever the caps**, since a still plan has no tail layers and the FFmpeg still draws no fades: `fades` / `transitions` / `keyed_*` are Motion abilities. The second half of a dip is drawn (nothing is live there; pinned by the transition parity family, both builds). `at(t)` is the frame on screen (`frame_containing`), not the nearest. Known costs and concerns: the index walk is `O(n)` for a track with one long clip under many short ones; Motion's per-frame `composite_matrix` is not FFmpeg 9's graph-wide negotiation (an A7 measurement: which matrix the export's conversion really takes over a cut whose bottom layer changes). A Motion plan holds **candidates** at a clip's closing edge (window closed at the end, as `between` has it); the pick decides which are drawn (`rendered.rs` pins that every drawn clip is planned and the only extras are on the closing frame). **Deferred**: the reframe's held `sendcmd` pose (item 8: sampled `pose` + `interp` today; the 0.05 degree gate, the four-decimal rounding and "an unmoving channel keeps the static value" are for the pass that draws a reframe, A5.6 — the schedule is `reframe_commands`' loop, which wants lifting into `clip_timing.rs` beside `Rational`).

**As landed in A1a-3** (`frame_pick.rs`, `media.rs`, `planner.rs`; differences from the sketch above): **Review follow-up (A1a-3b):** a still image is resolved through `Pick::Fps` too (`FpsPick.image: Option<Rational>`, finding 20; `PickRate` now refuses a mismatched rate for any layer); `SourceFrames` gained `last_duration` (finding 21) and `SourceFrames::NONE`; `generate_proxy` no longer writes a sidecar when a concurrent generator's proxy is already in place (`finalize_proxy` checks `dst` first and writes the sidecar only for the proxy it renames in), and each sidecar writer has its own temp name; `SpanPlan::handover` returns `Handover { stream_start, first_shown, frame }` where `frame` is the delivery frame of the whole cut — **in a project with no delivery frame the canvas derives from the clips on the timeline, so a slice that drops the clip that defined it is cut for another frame; the caller must pin `frame` as `ExportOptions::resolution` for the stream** (an A4 constraint, tested); `SourceMedia::decoded` carries the original's projection onto the proxy's stream (the proxy file has no spherical metadata of its own, and the clip's `reframe` is what a plan refuses on, so this was cosmetic, but a 360 asset's plan layer no longer reads flat). Alpha fades (finding 19) are not modelled. `Pick { AtOrAfter(f64), Before(f64), Fps(FpsPick) }` with `FpsPick { speed, reverse, window /* clip_source_window, tail included */, start, frame, fps /* pick_fps */, drop_first }` — self-contained, so `PlanTiming` keeps only the `enable` window and what A1a-2 put there. **`fps_pick(&FpsPick, &SourceFrames { pts, time_base, start_us, last_duration }) -> Option<usize>` does not resolve to a `Before(bound)` query** (finding 16): the answer is a monotone predicate over the file's frame slots, computed in ticks, so a `FrameSource` has to hold timestamps in ticks plus the time base and the container's start, and implement the same arithmetic over its index (`partition_point` over the kept frames; the pure function is its reference and its test oracle). `Before(T)` / `AtOrAfter(T)` remain the *queries* the cache answers (`Pick::select` implements both over a slice, with `-ss`'s tick rounding); `None` is "no frame": the clip is not drawn there, which is the whole of the closing-edge exactness (finding 17), and the first-frame loss at `k*(1/fps)` is `ClipTiming::enabled` at `ffmpeg_frame_time` (A1a-2). Reversed VFR is **not** refused: `reverse` re-stamps the forward timestamps in order, which the pick reproduces exactly (rendered). A rate whose canvas and `fps=` rationals differ is refused in Motion (`Unsupported::PickRate`): one grid is assumed. `PlanRequest<'a> { mode, color, media: &dyn MediaResolver }` (`still`/`motion` constructors default to `OriginalMedia`; `with_media`), `SourceMedia { path, proxy, video }` **without `identity`** (A1b-1's `source_identity` adds it) and `PlanLayer.source: PlanSource { proxy }`; the Planner builds the decoded asset list once, so `ClipFx.hdr` / `head_pad` follow the opened file while the canvas still derives from the originals. The proxy sidecar (`<hash>.json`, `ProxySidecar`: version, proxy byte size, the proxy's video `StreamInfo`) is written by `generate_proxy` *before* the rename, read by `proxy_video_info`, with **one** cached `ffprobe` fallback written back (not "resolves to the original while a background probe backfills": the fallback is synchronous and off the lock, the original is the answer only when even that fails; `proxy_path`'s own cached `source_traits` probe of the original is unchanged). `span`: `Planner::span` (eager RLE, `SpanPlan::runs`), `Planner::first_unsupported` (the lazy short-circuit; `SpanPlan::first_unsupported` answers from the runs), `SpanPlan::handover(a) -> Handover { stream_start, first_shown }` over the windows of finding 18, windows chaining. Tests: `picked.rs` (both FFmpegs: 5 sweeps of 128 / 288 / 90 / 120 clips + the dissolve tail + `proxy/late-video-start`, mutation-checked: dropping `drop_first` or rounding in `setpts` fails them), `rendered.rs` upgraded to "drawn == the pick names a frame", planner tests for picks, a resolved proxy, spans, the hand-over (a slice at the hand-over equals the full cut's fade / dissolve / tail state; one started inside a dissolve does not) and a 500-clip shape guard plus the `KERF_BENCH` line.

`fps_pick` (pure, kerf-core) resolves `Fps` to a query: forward = `Before(bound)` (finding 4); reverse = that rule over the window, mirrored (exact on constant rate; reversed VFR is refused). **Superseded by A1a-3 above: it is arithmetic over ticks (findings 16 and 17), and reversed VFR is exact.** The export cursor of a reversed clip buffers its window as the `reverse` filter does, up to a byte cap, and serves it mirrored; a longer window is refused (FFmpeg renders it). `FadeStep::progress_at_frame(k, fps)` is the frame-counting model of finding 12 (`LayerFx.fades` carries the steps, not a precomputed number); A5.1 ports `fade`'s per-frame integer arithmetic for the GPU ramp (the 8-bit rounding the model leaves out). Masks are fractions of the *layer* frame.

**`SourceMedia` fixes finding 7.** `SourceMedia { path, identity, proxy, video: StreamInfo }` is what decoding `path` yields; `trait MediaResolver` has `OriginalMedia` (A0) and `ProxyMedia`, which reads a **`StreamInfo` sidecar (`<hash>.json`) that `generate_proxy` writes after the encode** (on its background thread). A proxy without one resolves to the original while a background probe backfills it: **no ffprobe runs on the interactive path**. The canvas still derives from the original's streams.

**`gpu_supported` as data.** `GpuCaps` (all false = A0; `effects` a per-kind bitset: blur, sharpen, grayscale, invert, vignette, chroma key) comes from `Compositor::caps()`. Reasons are a **pure function of plan fields**, `RenderPlan::reasons(&GpuCaps, size) -> Vec<Unsupported>` (an enum whose `Display` keeps today's messages), not strings stored while planning; `gpu_supported_at` is `reasons(..).is_empty()`. An A5 pass is a caps flip beside the pass and its parity cases:

| item | plan carries | GPU today | until |
|---|---|---|---|
| layers, transform, crop, fit, `eq`, opacity, stills, sampled keyframes | yes | yes (A0) | |
| fades, dips, dissolve, slide/push, tails, Motion picks | `LayerFx`, `Pick` | refused | A5.1 |
| mask / text / effects+chroma / reframe / HDR | `mask` / `PlanText` / `effects` / `reframe` / `hdr` | refused | A5.2 to A5.6 |
| non-bicubic scaler, shrink > 40:1, non-4:2:0 resize, unknown matrix, non-8-bit-4:2:0 delivery | A0 reasons | refused | unchanged |

**`Planner` and `span`.** `Planner::new` computes `for_render`, the asset map, `transition_fx`, geometry and reframe schedules once, plus a **per-track sorted clip index**, so `at` is O(log n + active). `span(a, b, size, caps)` evaluates every grid frame and run-length-encodes `reasons(..).is_empty()`; **`first_unsupported(a, limit)` short-circuits** at the first bad frame within `limit` seconds (A4 scans 5 s ahead of the playhead; a bench line pins `at` on a 500-clip timeline). Per frame, not breakpoints: support flips *inside* a keyframed span (opacity through 1.0, scale through 40:1).

**Hand-over to the stream.** `SpanPlan::handover(a) -> (stream_start, first_shown)`: `stream_preview` plays `Timeline::slice(start, end)`, which drops outgoing tails and clears `transition_in` of a front-cut clip, so a stream started inside a transition would cut it. `stream_start` is the latest time <= `a` with no transition or fade window open; the callback drops frames before `first_shown = a`. A4 plans the *unsliced* timeline. `for_render` / `slice` / `for_delivery` otherwise stay caller-side transforms, as `build_export_args_phase` and `render_variants` apply them before the graph.

## 5. Risks, and out of A1

- **showinfo is a log channel**, verified on 6.1.1 and 9.0.2 only: self-test, `n:` pairing, fatal `NOPTS` and the disabled flag bound it; a build without it disables the path, never the app.
- **Which source frame** is a contract (`-ss` pick for A0/A3, the `fps` rule for A7); VFR proxies and late video starts are FFmpeg's own preview mismatch (finding 6), pinned by fixtures.
- **Memory and bandwidth**: a 4K frame is 12 MB (~370 MB/s at 30 fps through the pipe); byte caps, <= 6 runs and proxies contain it.
- **Out**: zero-copy hardware frames; surfaces (A2); the audio-clock loop (A4); GPU passes for fades, masks, text, effects, reframe, HDR (A5; A1 carries the data and refuses); P010/16-bit; GPU pooling and upload cache (A2/A3); chunked backward GOP decode for reverse; FFmpeg's still drawing fades; replacing `stream_preview`; disk frame caches; any change to export argv.
