What the table shows: FFmpeg pays about 650 ms more a still (and 150 ms more a frame of an export) for a feathered ellipse mask, because geq evaluates the expression for every sample of every frame; the compositor pays the plane once (1.5 ms at 960x540, 8.8 ms at 1080p) and a mask adds about 90 ms of blend on lavapipe (the per-sample alpha in the blend). 960 px renders scale the 1080p source (two swscale passes). The compositor is slower than FFmpeg on a CPU adapter for blur, sharpen and the moving zoom (a four-plane second stage, per-row compute passes); a real adapter is needed for the verdict.
