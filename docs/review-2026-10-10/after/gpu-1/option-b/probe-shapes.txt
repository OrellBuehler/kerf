What Project::probe_asset gives for real files (tools/mkshapes.sh, 1280x720; DV is 720x576). Identical on FFmpeg 9.0.2 and 6.1.1 except ffv1-rgb.mkv's tag (gbr on 9.0.2, none on 6.1.1).
Tags that are the decoder's default, not the file's word: gbr on every PNG (rgb24, rgba, gray) and on RGB video; bt470bg on JPEG, WebP and MJPEG video.
