GPU-1 proxy colour normalisation: before / after
Mesa lavapipe. FFmpeg 9.0.2 (pinned) = policy BottomLayerTag; FFmpeg 6.1.1 (system) = FixedBt601.

Refusals among the 15: 11 -> 5 (the 6 stacks of an untagged HD proxy beside a BT.709 or tone-mapped one,
plain or translucent, are no longer refused). The five that stay refused are other matrices (BT.601-class
or BT.2020 against BT.709): FFmpeg 9 converts those layers into the bottom layer's matrix with arithmetic
the compositor does not reproduce, and they stay on the FFmpeg path.
Deliberate regression: an untagged HD proxy beside a BT.601-class proxy (SD, smpte170m, an RGB capture) was
drawn (both read as BT.601) and is now refused (BT.709 vs BT.601), which is the right reading of it.

3. FFmpeg 6.1.1 (FixedBt601)
No stack is ever refused for its matrices. All 15 stacks drawn, flat PSNR 43.6 - 51.0 dB, max error 4 - 6; the
frames are the ones they were before (the label moves no pixel and 6.1.1 ignores tags).

4. Existing suites

kerf-core unit tests 1114; `-- --ignored` 91 pass on 9.0.2 (the one failure needs network: tts model download).
